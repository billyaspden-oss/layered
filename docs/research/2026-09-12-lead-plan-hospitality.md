# Lead plan — hospitality vertical

**Date:** 12 September 2026
**Companion to:** `2026-09-12-explee-lead-quality.md`
**Published as:** https://claude.ai/code/artifact/7b92228c-d364-4cf9-a7f3-98619978ac2d

Layered has one live AI reference: Plato at Gilpin. The buyers who look like
Gilpin are countable, clustered within an hour of Kendal, and currently
spending money. This is a finite named list worked warmly, not a funnel.

Routes are ranked by how warm the first contact is, because at a price point
of thousands, warmth is what converts.

---

## Route 1 (warmest) — Gilpin itself. Time-sensitive.

**Chris Cooper** became GM of Gilpin Hotel & Lake House in late January 2026,
replacing **Thomas Ferrante**, who had run the property since June 2024.
Cooper came from The Grand, York; before that Lucknam Park, Rockliffe Hall,
Whatley Manor. Boutique Hotelier named him Manager of the Month, August 2026.

Two consequences:

- **Risk.** If Plato was commissioned under Ferrante, Cooper inherited a
  system he did not choose. That is what gets dropped at budget review.
- **Opportunity.** A five-star GM who likes your system, has a fresh trade
  profile, and a career spanning four other luxury properties is the best
  introduction route available.

**Actions:** go in person, go to listen, leave with three asks — a case-study
quote, an introduction to the LDHA (route 2), and *one* named peer.

**Check first:** who signed off Plato, and has Cooper been properly briefed
on it. If not, that is the meeting.

## Route 2 — Lake District Hotels Association (LDHA)

Around 50 leading independent and branded properties and attractions across
Cumbria. Members employ 2,000+ people, offer 2,000+ bedrooms nightly, and
account for an estimated £459m direct and indirect economic impact.

**The point:** alongside its members and management groups, the LDHA runs a
standing **People Group** focused on member development, meeting regularly at
member venues. Its own framing is that well-coached people are members' most
precious resource. Layered built a staff training platform for a five-star
member. This is a pre-qualified room.

**Chair since June 2026: Michael Coletta**, MD of Langdale Leisure (Langdale
Hotel & Spa, Brimstone). He is both the association route and a multi-property
target. Predecessor: Ben Mayou, formerly Lakeside Hotel, now Ulverston Golf Club.

**Play:** ask Cooper for the introduction; offer the People Group a session
(what Gilpin learned putting eight departments' SOPs into one system,
including what failed), not a pitch.

**Unverified:** membership quoted as both "over 40" and "around 50";
headcount as both 2,000 and 3,000. Full member list at `ldhavoice.co.uk`
could not be opened during research — pull it before planning around names.

## Route 3 — Gilpin's own consortia

Gilpin belongs to **PoB Hotels** (Pride of Britain, 50+ independent UK hotels)
and **Relais & Châteaux**. Shared membership is a credential you can lead a
letter with.

Two PoB members in reach:

- **Northcote**, Langho, Ribble Valley — Craig Bancroft and Nigel Haworth.
  Michelin star every year since 1996. ~320 staff across hotel, cookery school,
  Ribble Valley Inns and café. **The strongest single target on this list**:
  at that spread of operations the knowledge problem is worse than Gilpin's.
- **Farlam Hall**, Brampton — Michelin star at The Cedar Tree, 13 rooms + 6
  stable suites.

Northcote also runs **Obsession**, an annual guest-chef festival since 1999
and the largest yearly gathering of northern chefs and hoteliers.

## What not to do

- **Don't buy a contact list.** See the Explee report. This document contains
  more usable names than that product would have returned.
- **Don't send volume cold email.** The reply rate that matters isn't
  reachable that way at this price point, and it contradicts "you deal
  directly with the person building your system".
- **Don't take the Freelancer work**, even when quiet. Every £400 WordPress
  job rebuilds the positioning the July reframe dismantled.
- **Don't widen the vertical yet.** One reference worked hard beats three
  verticals with nothing to show.

## Before emailing anyone

UK PECR treats sole traders and non-LLP partnerships as individual
subscribers: they need consent. Limited companies and LLPs do not. The groups
above are almost all limited companies; some smaller independents may not be.
Companies House is free. Since February 2026 the maximum penalty matches
UK GDPR.

## The weakness in this plan

Concentrated: one vertical, one geography, one reference that changed hands
eight months ago. If Cooper doesn't engage, route 1 disappears and routes 2
and 3 lose much of their force, because the Gilpin story is what makes an
approach to a peer hotel land.

So: make the Cooper conversation happen within a fortnight, and get a second
AI reference public. Boat HQ named and live would do it, if the owner agrees.
